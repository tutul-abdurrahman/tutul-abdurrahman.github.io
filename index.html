04 — Process

Three steps. No mystery.

Step 01

15-minute scope call

What the product is, who uses it, and the one action the screen has to support. I ask the questions that decide the build.

Step 02

Flows first, then screens

You see the structure before the pixels. First real screens within 48 hours, in a file you can click through on your phone.

Step 03

Build, handoff, support

I build it or hand it off with annotated specs. Either way you get the source, a short guide, and a month of small fixes.

About

I'm Abdur Rahman Tutul, a product designer in Dhaka. For the last three years I've worked with Rebelslab in the Netherlands and Zephyr Events, designing course platforms, election guides, event dashboards and the marketing sites around them.

A lot of that work is unglamorous on purpose: the admin screen where a client uploads a course, the integration bridge that keeps Swoogo and a CRM in sync, the CMS a non-technical team has to use without calling me. That's usually where a product succeeds or fails.

I design in Figma and Miro, build in HTML, CSS and Tailwind, and hand off with enough detail that a developer doesn't have to guess. I also build self-initiated pages to keep the craft honest — 18 of them are live on this site.

03 — Process

Three steps. No mystery.

Step 01

15-minute call

You tell me the product, the audience and the one action the page must cause. I ask the questions your last designer didn't.

Step 02

Draft in 48 hours

A real page at a real URL, not a screenshot. You click through it on your phone before we go further. Direction changes are cheap here.

Step 03

Build & ship

Revisions, responsive pass, performance and accessibility check. Then it goes on your domain. I stay reachable for a month for small fixes.

About

I'm Abdur Rahman Tutul, a UI/UX designer in Dhaka. I spent three years on product workflows — wireframes, user flows, design systems — and kept running into the same wall: the design looked right in Figma and died in the build.

So I started building them myself. Now I design and ship in one pass: layout, type, colour, then actual HTML and CSS, tested on real devices before it goes out. The 18 pages on this site are proof, not a promise.

I work with small businesses, solo founders and studios. I'm direct about what a page can and cannot do, and I don't sell a redesign when the real problem is the offer.

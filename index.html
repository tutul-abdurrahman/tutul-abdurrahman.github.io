Process

How it actually runs.

Most sites take 10 to 14 days from scope to live. Product work runs longer, in weekly slices you can see.

Day 1

One call, then a written scope

Pages, states, what is out, price and dates on one page. You approve it before I open Figma. No kickoff deck.

Days 2 to 4

Flows first, then a real screen

Sitemap and flows, then a clickable screen on your phone. I design the empty, loading, error and admin states too.

Days 5 to 12

Built in the browser

Responsive HTML and CSS or your CMS, on a staging URL that updates while I work. You comment on the site, not a ZIP.

After launch

Repo, guide, 30 days

Source and CMS access, a one-page handover, and a month of small fixes. Then hourly or a retainer, your call.

About

I am Abdur Rahman Tutul, a product designer in Dhaka. For the last three years I have worked with Rebelslab in the Netherlands, Zephyr and Uniloop, and with SR Web Studio closer to home, designing course platforms, election guides, event dashboards and the marketing sites around them.

A lot of that work is unglamorous on purpose: the admin screen where a client uploads a course, the integration bridge that keeps Swoogo and a CRM in sync, the CMS a non-technical team has to use without calling me. That is usually where a product succeeds or fails.

I design in Figma and Miro, build in HTML, CSS and Tailwind, and hand off with enough detail that a developer does not have to guess. When a project needs an integration, I do not just draw the screen. I wire it up and test it against the real API.

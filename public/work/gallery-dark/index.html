About

I photograph people the way they actually are, not the way they were told to stand.

I started with a borrowed 50mm and a cousin's wedding nobody else wanted to shoot. Seven years later the method has not changed: stay quiet, stay close enough to be ignored, and wait for the second after the pose falls apart. That second is usually the photograph.

Almost everything here is available light, a window, a doorway, a street lamp doing more work than a softbox would. I shoot portrait sessions, full weddings, and editorial commissions, and I deliver edited frames rather than a hard drive of maybes. If a picture looks staged, I have failed it.

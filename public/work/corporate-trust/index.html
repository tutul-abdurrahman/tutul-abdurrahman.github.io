How an engagement runs

Three phases, one partner.

Most projects run six to twelve weeks. The rhythm is the same whether the question is a merger or a management team.

Discover

Two weeks of interviews, data and site visits. We write down the question you are actually trying to answer, which is often not the one in the brief.

Weeks one and two

Design

We test the options with the people who will have to run them. Models, workshops and a short paper that shows the trade-offs plainly.

Weeks three to six

Deliver

You get the recommendation, the board pack and the first ninety days of the plan. We stay for the first review, at no extra cost.

Weeks seven to twelve

Retainers

Clear fees, no surprise bills.

Amounts are monthly and exclusive of travel. Every retainer includes a partner and an analyst, not a team of six.

Questions

Straight answers, as always.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within two weeks. We deliberately keep capacity free for one urgent engagement at a time, so tell us the board date and we will say honestly whether we can make it.

Do you work with companies under $10M revenue?

Yes, through the advice retainer. Below that size the engagement model is usually too heavy, and we will tell you so on the first call rather than sell you one.

Will you sign an NDA before the first call?

Happily. Send yours or use ours. We will not ask for a data room before we have agreed the question and the fee.

Can you work alongside our existing advisers?

Often that is the best arrangement. We are clear about who owns which workstream and we do not bill for time spent managing the other firms.

Do you take equity or success fees?

Rarely. We prefer a fixed fee agreed up front. Where a success fee makes sense, it is capped and disclosed in the engagement letter.

Your next board decision deserves a partner who stays.

Tell us the question and the date. We will reply within one working day with a view on fit, a partner name and a fee range.

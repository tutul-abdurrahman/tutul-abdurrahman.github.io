Our way

Buy less wood. Buy the right wood.

Nordbo started in 2014 with one bench design and a stubborn rule: if a piece cannot survive twenty Danish winters and two moves, it does not leave the workshop. We build in birch and oak we can trace to the stand of forest it grew in, finish with plant-based oils, and cover every joint for ten years. Nothing is lacquered to look finished, it is finished to be lived on. Sofas get nap-marks; tables get pencil rings; that is the point, and we design for it.

The craft

Three materials, no shortcuts.

Every Nordbo piece is a small set of honest materials doing hard work. These are the three we build our name on.

Solid birch, traceable to the stand

Each tabletop carries the forest region on its underside label, 4 regions in Finland and Sweden, all FSC-certified. Birch is air-dried 18 months before we cut it, so boards stay flat for decades.

Wool from six family farms

Blankets and upholstery come from six mills in Jutland and Vestfold that card, spin and weave on site. Nothing is chemical-softened; lambswool warms whether the radiator argues with the rain or not.

Repairable by hand

Every joint is a dowel or a bolt, no glue-only corners. Replacement legs, slats and covers ship from Randers within 2 days, and a folded wrench lives in the flat-pack's wood cert note.

Why it lasts

Six things a Nordbo home does differently.

Warmer evenings

Lambswool throws and blush-toned lamps hold candlelight better, 74% of review surveys describe a room re-lit "softer" after switching textiles.

Zero VOC mornings

Plant-oil finishes, not lacquer. Rooms smell of wood, not chemistry, a common note in allergy-house orders.

Flat-pack without the fight

Fewer than 8 bolts per average piece; the largest build we ship needs one hex key and 25 minutes on tape, not 3 hours.

Buys that hold value

Nordbo pieces resell for ~62% of list after 3 years on the second-hand listings we track, the market knows Bolig for birch, and asks for it by name.

Covers that outgrow stains

Every sofa ships with a spare cover wash-cycled; wool self-cleans with air and brushing rather than a solvent bath.

A ten-year straight answer

The repair promise is paper, not apps: one phone line, human pickup in Denmark, Germany, the Netherlands and the UK.

How it's made

From forest stand to your hallway, in 3 moves.

Cut to the forest's schedule

Boards are sorted in Randers by season of felling; the winter cut is denser and book-matched for tops, the softer summer cut becomes legs and rails.

Assemble with what unscrews

Dowels and bolts only, jigs drilled to a 0.1 mm tolerance so a replacement rail from 2031 still seats perfectly in today's seat frame.

Oil, wrap, doorstep

Two coats of linseed-plus-beeswax, a wool interleaf wrap, and carbon-counted delivery with a 2-hour window you can move from the tracking page.

Help

Asked often, answered plainly.

Building is optional. Every bundle includes assembly add-on at checkout (from $45/piece in our delivery regions); outside those, the packs average 25 minutes and need one hex key, which is taped inside the wood-identification note.

Structural joints, legs, rails, seat frames and slats, any part that holds weight or shape. Oil and cleaning are yours; replacement parts are ours. After 10 years we keep parts in print for another 5, usually at cost.

Live with the piece for 60 days. If the room disagrees, we collect it from your floor, refund to card within 5 working days, and the return is re-oiled and re-sold as "Second Bloom" at a 20% discount, never landfilled.

Dust with a dry cloth; re-oil once a year (a 100 ml bottle ships with every top, enough for 4 coats). Spills are fine if wiped the same day. Cochairs and desk tops 3, 4 and 7 inches deep ship pre-oiled from spring 2025.

Nine countries: Denmark, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, France, Sweden, Norway, Finland and the UK. Stocked pieces leave Randers in 2–4 working days; made-to-order wool colours add 2 weeks. Delivery windows move in the tracking page, free.

Yes, the Randers showroom keeps every bundle staged as one finished room, open Tue–Sat 10–18. Berlin and Amsterdam host rotating pop-ups about every 6 months; the Journal lists dates as they are confirmed.

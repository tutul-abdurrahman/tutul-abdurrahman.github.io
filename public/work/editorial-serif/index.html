About

Nine years of reading the file twice.

Nine years of practice in Dhaka have mostly been spent on property and family matters, where the paperwork decides the argument. The first job with any file is to read it slowly, then tell the client the plain version, what is strong, what is missing, and what the next step costs in time.

Mahmudul Karim works in Bangla and English, keeps written notes of every advice given, and prefers a settlement that holds to a fight that only looks like a win. Where a matter has to go to court, it is prepared as if it will be tried.

Questions

Before the first meeting.

What should I bring to a first consultation?

Bring any notices, deeds, contracts and messages, plus a short timeline of what happened. Photocopies are enough for the first meeting; bring originals only when they are specifically needed.

How long does a property dispute usually take?

It depends on the records and on whether the other side is willing to settle. Some partition matters close in months; contested title suits can take years. You will get a realistic range after the papers are read, not before.

Do you take matters outside Dhaka?

Matters in Dhaka are the main practice. Travel elsewhere in Bangladesh is possible for hearings and meetings by prior arrangement, and is discussed before any engagement begins.

How are fees set?

Fees depend on the scope and stage of the matter. You receive a written estimate before work begins, and costs such as court fees and travel are listed separately so there are no surprises.

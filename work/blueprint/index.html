GENERAL NOTES — SHEET 04

How this office works

Five notes. They apply to every drawing, every fee, every site visit. If one of them ever costs you a day, the other five will save you a month.

  1. All figures carry units. Member sizes, loads and tolerances are stated on the drawing, not in an email. A number without a unit is not issued.
  2. One engineer owns your job. From survey to construction issue, the same chartered engineer signs your sheets. No handover to a tier of account managers.
  3. No undrawable geometry. We do not model what we cannot build. Every curve on our drawings has a setting-out method a site crew can execute with a tape and a string line.
  4. RFIs answered in 240 minutes or less during working hours — measured, logged monthly, and the figure has held since 2023.
  5. Fees are fixed per stage. Appraisal, scheme, detail design, construction issue. Change the scope and we re-quote; otherwise the number holds.

A-02 — REPLY, REV C Site survey within 10 working days of instruction for standard frames; 15 for occupied or live-logistics sites. We hold two mobilisation slots open per month and will confirm the exact date in the fee proposal.
A-03 — REPLY, REV C One chartered engineer (CEng, MIStructE) is named as engineer of record on your title block and stays with the job end to end. The signature block on every sheet matches the name in your contract.

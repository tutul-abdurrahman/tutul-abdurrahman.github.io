WHY PORTIONING

Grocery shopping asks you to buy a bunch of cilantro for one garnish and guess how much rice a family of four actually eats. Morsel does the guessing once, in a kitchen with a scale. Every compartment holds exactly what the recipe needs, so the half-wilted bunch in your crisper drawer simply stops existing.

Five compartments, zero guesswork

Every kit opens the same tidy way. Once you've cooked three Morsel nights, your hands know the tray before your eyes do.

THE INGREDIENT WELLS

Pre-portioned, vacuum-tidied, in the order you cook them

Compartment 1 holds the protein, the smallest wells hold the spices. Open left to right and the recipe writes itself, our test cooks averaged 4 minutes from box to stove, measured across 600 deliveries in August 2026.

30-minute recipes, timed to the tray

Every card is written for 5 steps and one pan. Median cook, start to plate: 29 minutes.

Six chefs rotate 68 recipes

A rotating cast of 6 working chefs adds 6 new recipes a month and retires the weakest performer.

Zero-waste portions

92% of ingredients arrive pre-measured. The remaining 8% (oils, salt, pepper) are your pantry's job.

Chilled chain, cold at your door

Insulated, curbside-recyclable liners held at 4°C in transit. Delivery-temp check prints on each box.

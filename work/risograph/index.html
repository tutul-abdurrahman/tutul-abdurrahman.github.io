Every run is a small mutiny against perfect printing.

A risograph is not a printer; it is a drum with opinions. Ink bleeds, registration drifts, the third copy of a run never matches the four hundredth. We stopped fighting that in our second month and started printing with it. The slip between the pink drum and the blue drum is 2px — we measure it, we keep it, and it is signed into every job sheet.

That is the whole philosophy. Two inks, one paper, no gradients and no glossy stock — the constraints are the aesthetic. When an art-director client asks us to "add a third color, make it smooth", we quote them double and a warning label. When they ask for misregistration kept, we harden the plates. Mimeo exists because someone, somewhere, still wants a page that smells like its own drum.
